## How billing is structured

Case fees are billed in three gates, matching the pipeline stages in
`pipeline-stages-overview.md`:

1. **Intake fee** — charged when a case is accepted at intake.
2. **Extraction and sequencing fee** — charged before a case enters the
   sequencing stage, covering both the extraction work already done and
   the sequencing work about to begin.
3. **Genealogy fee** — charged before a case enters the genealogy
   stage, only for cases where genealogy research is elected.

A case that is never elected for genealogy is only ever billed the
first two gates.

## Payment methods by account type

- **Institutional accounts** (law-enforcement agencies, medical
  examiner and coroner's offices) are invoiced on **Net-30 terms** —
  payment is due 30 days from invoice date, typically against a
  purchase order or case-fund authorization.
- **Individual/family accounts** pay by credit card at each billing
  gate through the client portal; a gate's stage does not begin until
  payment is confirmed.

## Late payment

For institutional Net-30 invoices, a case is not paused for a late
payment on its own — casework continues while accounts payable is
resolved between our billing team and the agency's finance office. For
individual accounts, a case pauses at the current billing gate until
payment is confirmed, since a card payment (not an invoice) triggers
gate progression.

## Billing disputes — always escalate

**Any billing dispute — a requester disputing a charge, an invoice
amount, a duplicate charge, or asking for a credit — must be escalated
to a human billing specialist. The agent must never adjudicate a
billing dispute, promise a credit or adjustment, or state that a charge
was or was not correct.** Acknowledge the concern, confirm we're
routing it to billing, and stop there. See
`escalation-and-specialist-requests.md`.

## Questions the agent can answer directly

Straightforward, non-disputed billing questions are fine to answer from
this document: what the three billing gates are, which gate a case's
current stage corresponds to, and general payment-method and Net-30
term information. The line is disagreement or a request for a monetary
adjustment — that always escalates.
