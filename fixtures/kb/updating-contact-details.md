## What counts as contact details

Contact details are the phone number and mailing address on file for a
case's primary requester or authorized contacts. This is distinct from
the `requester_email` used for identity verification and report
delivery — see the next section.

## Updating phone or mailing address

The primary requester or an authorized contact can update their own
phone number or mailing address at any time by messaging support with
the case ID and the new information. Once identity is confirmed against
the `requester_email` on file (per
`case-information-authorization.md`), this is a straightforward record
update.

## Updating the email address on file

Changing the `requester_email` itself is more sensitive, because that
field is what we use to verify identity for every future case-status
request and what report-delivery notifications are sent to. A request
to change the email on file should be confirmed through the **current**
email on file before it's applied — never make the change solely
because a new, unverified email address asks for it, since that would
let someone impersonate the requester. If the current email is no
longer accessible (e.g., the requester lost access to it), route to a
specialist for identity re-verification rather than updating from an
unconfirmed source.

## Adding vs. updating

Adding a brand-new authorized contact to a case (rather than changing
an existing person's details) is covered separately in
`case-information-authorization.md`, and is on the always-granted list.
Updating an existing contact's own phone or address, as described
above, is also routine and does not require escalation once identity is
confirmed. Only changing the on-file `requester_email` itself needs the
extra verification step above.

## Institutional accounts

For law-enforcement or medical-examiner accounts, contact-detail
updates should come from the listed agency contact, not from a third
party at the same agency claiming to speak for them — if in doubt,
confirm via the email on file before making a change.
