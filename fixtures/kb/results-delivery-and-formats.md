## When a report is delivered

A report is generated and delivered once a case reaches the
**complete** stage (`eta_weeks: 0`). Delivery happens automatically —
requesters do not need to request their report once their case is
complete.

## Delivery channel

Reports are posted to the requester's secure Meridian client portal
account and an email notification is sent to the `requester_email` on
file for the case, with a link to sign in and download. We do not
email report attachments directly, since case reports can contain
sensitive genetic and case information.

## Available formats

- **Full Technical Report (PDF)** — the complete deliverable: profile
  summary, methodology notes, chain-of-custody documentation, and, if
  elected, the genealogy findings and candidate leads. This is the
  default format for law-enforcement and medical examiner accounts.
- **Summary Letter (PDF)** — a condensed, plain-language version
  covering the outcome and headline findings without full technical
  methodology detail. Available on request, most often used for family
  requesters.
- **Investigative Lead Package** — for cases with genealogy research,
  an additional package aimed at investigators: candidate family trees,
  match evidence, and suggested next investigative steps. Provided
  alongside the Full Technical Report, never as a replacement for it.

## Requesting a different format or an additional copy

A requester can ask for the Summary Letter version of an already
completed Full Technical Report, or ask us to resend the portal
notification email, at any time after their case reaches complete.

**Resending a copy of an already-delivered report to the email address
on file for the case is an always-granted request** — it does not
require escalation or additional approval. See
`case-information-authorization.md` for the full list of always-granted
requests and the identity checks that still apply.

## What the report does not certify

As described in `dna-profiles-and-genealogy-research.md`, genealogy
findings are investigative leads, not a certified legal identification.
The report states this distinction explicitly; do not characterize a
delivered report as a final identification when responding to a
requester — describe it as the completed lab and/or genealogy findings
for their case.

## If a report seems wrong or incomplete

Requesters who believe the delivered report has an error, is missing a
section, or disagree with a finding should be escalated — see
`escalation-and-specialist-requests.md`. Do not attempt to reinterpret
or amend report contents yourself.
