## What data we hold

For every case, Meridian Forensic Genomics holds: chain-of-custody and
submission records, extraction and sequencing lab data, the resulting
DNA profile (when generated), accession photographs, genealogy research
materials (when elected), and the final report. This is sensitive
genetic and case data, and it is handled under stricter controls than
ordinary customer records.

## Who can access case data

Access is restricted to the case's authorized parties — see
`case-information-authorization.md` for exactly who qualifies and how
identity is verified before any case detail is shared.

## Retention period

Case files, profiles, and reports are retained for **7 years** after
report delivery (i.e., 7 years from when a case reaches `complete`).
Cases tied to an active law-enforcement investigation are retained
**indefinitely**, at the submitting agency's instruction, regardless of
the standard 7-year window.

## Extending the retention window

A requester or submitting agency may ask us to extend the standard
retention window before it lapses — for example, because an
investigation is still open but not formally flagged as active in our
system yet. **Extending a case's retention window by up to 12 months at
a time is an always-granted request** — see
`case-information-authorization.md` for the full always-granted list.
Extensions beyond 12 months, or a request to make a case's retention
indefinite, should be routed to a specialist to confirm active-case
status with the submitting agency.

## Early deletion requests

A request to delete case data before the standard retention window has
elapsed is not something the agent can grant on its own, even from the
primary requester, because evidentiary cases may be subject to a legal
hold from the submitting agency that supersedes an individual
requester's preference. Route these to a human specialist — see
`escalation-and-specialist-requests.md`.

## Data sharing

We do not sell, publish, or otherwise share case data outside the
authorized parties on a case. Genetic profiles are only submitted to
opt-in genealogy databases as described in
`dna-profiles-and-genealogy-research.md`, and only for cases where
genealogy research has been elected by the submitting agency or
requester.

## Security

Case data is encrypted at rest and in transit, and portal access
requires authenticated login tied to the case's authorized contacts.
Report delivery never happens via unauthenticated email attachment —
see `results-delivery-and-formats.md`.
