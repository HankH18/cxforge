## The primary requester

Every case has one primary requester, recorded as `requester_name` and
`requester_email` on the case. This is either the submitting
law-enforcement/medical-examiner agency contact (for evidentiary cases)
or the family member who initiated the case (for reference-sample
cases). The primary requester can always ask about their own case's
status, stage, and general timeline once we've matched their inbound
contact email to the `requester_email` on file.

## Authorized additional contacts

A primary requester may add other people to a case as authorized
contacts — for example, a second family member, or a co-investigator at
a partner agency. Once added, an authorized contact can request case
status the same as the primary requester. Authorized contacts are not
automatically able to redirect report delivery or change the primary
requester on the case; those require the primary requester's own
request or specialist review.

## Adding an authorized contact — always granted

**Adding a new authorized contact to a case, at the request of the
existing primary requester, is an always-granted request.** The agent
should process it directly once the request comes from the email
already on file as the case's primary requester — no escalation
needed.

## The full always-granted list

To keep routine, low-risk requests fast, the following are always
granted by the agent directly, without escalation, once the requester's
identity matches the case's `requester_email` on file:

1. **Adding an authorized contact** to a case (this document).
2. **Resending a copy of an already-delivered report** to the email
   address on file (`results-delivery-and-formats.md`).
3. **Extending a case's records-retention window by up to 12 months**
   (`privacy-and-data-retention.md`).

Anything not on this list — including changing the primary requester,
redirecting delivery to a new email, early data deletion, billing
adjustments, or retention extensions beyond 12 months — requires
specialist review; see `escalation-and-specialist-requests.md`.

## Requests from someone not on the case

If an inbound message's email does not match the `requester_email` on
file and is not a recorded authorized contact, do not confirm or deny
any case details, including whether a case with that name exists.
Explain that we can only discuss a case with its authorized contacts,
and offer to have the primary requester add them as an authorized
contact if that's appropriate. Third-party requests that push back on
this (e.g., media, unrelated relatives, or anyone citing legal
authority) should be escalated rather than argued with — see
`escalation-and-specialist-requests.md`.

## Updating who a case's contact information belongs to

For changing the primary requester's own contact details (phone,
mailing address, or the email address itself), see
`updating-contact-details.md`.
