## General principle

Refund eligibility follows the same three billing gates described in
`billing-and-payment-terms.md`: whether a fee is refundable depends on
whether the lab work it paid for has already started, not simply on
whether the requester asks before the case finishes.

## Intake fee

The intake fee is fully refundable if the requester withdraws the case
**within 5 business days of submission and before the case leaves the
intake stage**. Once intake work is complete and the case moves to
extraction, the intake fee is no longer refundable, since the
accessioning and chain-of-custody review work has been performed.

## Extraction and sequencing fee

Once this fee is charged and extraction has begun, it is
**non-refundable** under normal withdrawal — reagents and instrument
time are consumed as soon as extraction starts, regardless of the
outcome. The one exception is unrecoverable sample failure: if a case
exhausts all extraction attempts under `sample-quality-and-reextraction.md`
and a replacement sample cannot be obtained or also fails, the portion
of this fee covering sequencing (which never occurred) is refunded,
while the extraction-work portion is not.

## Genealogy fee

The genealogy fee is fully refundable if a requester withdraws
genealogy research **before the genealogy stage begins** (i.e., while
the case is still in sequencing or earlier). Once the genealogy team
has started research, this fee is non-refundable regardless of whether
the research ultimately produces a strong lead, a weak lead, or no
lead — see `genealogy-limitations-and-expectations.md` for why a
"no result" outcome does not indicate the work wasn't performed.

## Rush surcharges

Rush surcharges (see `requesting-a-rush.md`) follow the fee they were
added to: refundable only under the same conditions as the underlying
extraction/sequencing fee.

## What the agent can do directly

The agent may explain this policy and tell a requester which category
their situation falls into. **Actually issuing a refund, or confirming
that a specific charge on a specific case will be refunded, is a
billing action and must be escalated** per
`billing-and-payment-terms.md` — explaining eligibility rules is not
the same as approving a refund.
